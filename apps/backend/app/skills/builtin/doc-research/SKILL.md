# Documentation research

- Restate the question in one line, then look for the answer in the project's own files first.
- Prefer official documentation and the version the project actually uses.
- Quote or cite the source for every claim; when sources disagree, say so.
- Say "I could not find this" rather than guessing, and list what you tried.
- End with a short answer and the one or two sources that matter most.
