# Scoping interview

- Read the idea and the project first; do not ask what you can see or sensibly assume.
- Ask at most four short, specific questions per turn, only about things that change what gets
  built: who uses it, the must-have features, look and feel, whether people sign in, what data
  it stores and where.
- Offer a default with each question ("I would assume X; is that right?") so the user can answer
  in one word.
- After two or three turns, or when the user says "go ahead", stop asking: write the plan and
  list your assumptions in the PRD.
- Never ask about technology unless the user cares; choose the stack yourself and say why in one line.
